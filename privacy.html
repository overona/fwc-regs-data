Privacy Policy — FL Saltwater Fishing Regs

Last updated 12 August 2026

FL Saltwater Fishing Regs is published by Verona Solutions, LLC. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It is short because the app collects very little.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no contact form. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, and we do not collect your location, photos, contacts, files or any other personal data.

What stays on your device

The app stores the regulation dataset it has downloaded, along with the version it holds and when it last checked for an update. All of this lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app. None of it is sent anywhere.

Regulation updates

To keep regulations current the app periodically requests a small file from overona.github.io, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web request this reveals your device's IP address and general software details to GitHub, which may log them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The request contains no identifier for you or your device, and we receive no report about who made it.

The app works fully offline. If you never connect it to a network, it makes no requests at all and simply uses the regulations bundled with it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To serve those ads, Google's advertising software may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. That collection is Google's, not ours — we never receive it, and we cannot identify you from it.

You can control this:

  • Android lets you reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Where local law requires a consent choice, the app asks you before ads are personalised, and you can change that answer later from the same prompt.

Google explains what it collects in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

A note on the regulations themselves

The regulation data is extracted from myfwc.com and reviewed before release, but it can be out of date or wrong. It is not legal advice. Always confirm with the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission before fishing.
