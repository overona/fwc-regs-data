IDMyFish — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026.

IDMyFish collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Everything the app does happens on your device.

Photos

When you take or choose a photo, it is analysed on your device by a fish-recognition model built into the app. The photo is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including us. The app does not request the Android camera permission; it uses the system camera and photo picker, which hand it only the single image you choose.

ID History

If you unlock IDMyFish Unlimited, you can save identifications to a history. The history — a small thumbnail of the fish, the species, the date and any note you type — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not synced or backed up by us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms. The app receives only the store's confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details. Restoring a purchase on a new device is likewise handled by the store.

Network use

The app makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity is (1) the store transaction above and (2) reference links (Wikipedia, iNaturalist, FishBase) that open in your browser only when you tap them; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

IDMyFish is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, we do not expect it to change materially.
