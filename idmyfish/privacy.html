IDMyFish — Privacy Policy

Effective 7 September 2026.

IDMyFish itself collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, and no server of its own. Everything the app does — including identifying your fish — happens on your device. The free version shows a small banner advertisement served by Google; that is the only part of the app that communicates with a server, and it is described below.

Photos

When you take or choose a photo, it is analysed on your device by a fish-recognition model built into the app. The photo is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including us or any advertising network. The app does not request the Android camera permission; it uses the system camera and photo picker, which hand it only the single image you choose.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version displays banner ads served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information (model, operating system, app version), and interactions with the ads themselves. Google uses this data as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not receive this data and cannot link it to you.

On iOS the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for consent before showing personalised ads; you can decline, in which case only non-personalised ads are shown. You can also limit ad personalisation for all apps in your device's privacy settings.

IDMyFish Unlimited removes ads entirely. After the one-time unlock the advertising SDK is not started, no consent or tracking prompt is shown, and the app makes no advertising requests.

ID History

If you unlock IDMyFish Unlimited, you can save identifications to a history. The history — a small thumbnail of the fish, the species, the date and any note you type — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is not synced or backed up by us, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms. The app receives only the store's confirmation that the purchase exists; it never sees your payment details. Restoring a purchase on a new device is likewise handled by the store.

Network use

Apart from advertising requests in the free version, the app makes no network requests of its own. The only other network activity is (1) the store transaction above and (2) reference links (Wikipedia, iNaturalist, FishBase) that open in your browser only when you tap them; those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children

IDMyFish is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
